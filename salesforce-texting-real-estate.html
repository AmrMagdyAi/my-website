Texting sellers from Salesforce: 10DLC, consent and tools.

To text sellers from Salesforce in the US, you need a texting tool that logs to Salesforce, a registered 10DLC brand and campaign, and consent and opt-out fields every tool respects. Here are the tools, the registration steps, what CTIA’s guidelines say about lists, and how I set up the fields. This is not legal advice.

Short version
  • Twilio: “Anyone sending SMS/MMS messages over a 10DLC number from an application to the US must register for A2P 10DLC.” Registration has a brand and a campaign. source
  • CTIA’s guidelines say marketing texts need express written consent and senders “should not use rented, sold, or shared lists.” That matters for skip-traced lists. source
  • Salesforce retired its standard SMS channels on February 14, 2026; US long code registration takes 1–4 weeks. source

What is 10DLC and do investors need it?

10DLC is the US system for business texting from regular 10-digit numbers. Twilio says anyone sending app-to-person texts over a 10DLC number to the US must register, including individuals. You register a brand (who is sending; standard brands need a business tax ID) and a campaign (the use case and your opt-in, opt-out and help messages). Registered traffic gets less filtering and more throughput. source

The Campaign Registry runs the registry behind it; brands register through a provider such as Twilio, not directly. source

CTIA’s document is voluntary industry guidance, not a substitute for TCPA advice. Talk to a lawyer before you text lists. source

How should Salesforce store texting data?

  • Consent: a checkbox plus date and source (“web form, October 2026”). No consent, no marketing texts.
  • Opt-out: one field every tool checks before sending, set automatically when someone replies STOP.
  • Every text on the timeline: calls and texts in one history, so the next rep sees the whole conversation.
  • DNC and litigator flags from your skip-tracing provider, if it returns them. BatchData’s API returns DNC and TCPA litigator checks. source

Questions

Do I need 10DLC registration to text sellers?

Twilio says anyone sending app-to-person SMS over a 10DLC number to the US must register for A2P 10DLC. Toll-free numbers and short codes are separate systems.

Can I text a skip-traced list from Salesforce?

CTIA’s guidelines call for express written consent for marketing texts and say senders should not use rented, sold or shared lists. Get legal advice on the TCPA before texting lists.

What happened to Salesforce’s standard SMS channels?

Salesforce retired standard SMS channels on February 14, 2026. New channels are enhanced, and US long code registration takes 1–4 weeks.

Can texts and calls show in one Salesforce timeline?

Yes, if your dialer or texting tool logs both to the same record. Dialpad logs SMS as completed tasks on the contact; RingCentral logs calls and messages.
