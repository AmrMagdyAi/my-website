Salesforce access for VAs and cold callers.

Real estate teams can give VAs and cold callers exactly the Salesforce access they need with profiles, permission sets, sharing rules and field-level security, and protect the lead list by removing export rights. Here’s what each role should see, which features do it, and a VA onboarding and offboarding checklist.

Short version
  • Most investor teams have VAs and cold callers, often in other countries. Salesforce lets you give them exactly the access they need: permission sets, sharing rules and field-level security. permission sets
  • Salesforce enforces multi-factor authentication for all employee logins; production enforcement rolled out July 20 to September 3, 2026. Plan it into VA onboarding. source
  • Field history tracking shows who changed what, for up to 20 fields per object. source

A VA onboarding and offboarding checklist

Before day one

Create the user with the caller profile and permission set; add to the right queue; confirm MFA method; share the call script and the handoff rules.

First week

Review their call outcomes daily; check the fields they skip; fix the layout before blaming the person.

Ongoing

Weekly calibration: compare their warm and hot leads with what acquisitions found.

Offboarding (same day)

Freeze then deactivate the user; reassign open leads and tasks to the queue; revoke integrations or dialer seats tied to them.

How do you protect your lead list?

  • Remove the export permission from caller and VA profiles; only owners export.
  • Give VAs list views, not report builder access, if they don’t need it.
  • Track changes to lead source, phone and owner with field history.
  • Keep integration users separate from people, so a departing VA can’t take an API connection with them. API access itself is included in Enterprise and an add-on for Professional. source

Questions

How do I give a VA limited access in Salesforce?

Give them a restricted profile plus a permission set with only what they need, hide sensitive fields with field-level security, and use queues and sharing rules so they see only their leads.

Does Salesforce require MFA for VAs?

Salesforce enforces multi-factor authentication for all employee logins, with production enforcement rolled out from July 20 to September 3, 2026.

Can I stop VAs exporting my leads?

Yes. Export is a permission; leave it off caller and VA profiles and permission sets so only owners can export.
