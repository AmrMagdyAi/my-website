The company in this sample: a home-buying team in two markets with 6 Salesforce users, acquisitions reps, a lead manager, a dispo lead and a transaction coordinator. Leads come from mail, PPC, cold calling and texting.

1. Summary for the owner

The org is in decent shape for day-to-day work. The money leaks sit in three places: missed calls on marketing numbers that nobody gets alerted on, offers that go quiet because nobody owns the next step, and campaign ROI that counts contracts instead of deals that closed. Fixing those three comes first.

3. The 30 / 60 / 90 roadmap

  • First 30 days: fix the missed-call alert, add the follow-up task on every offer, and add the duplicate rule on phone. These stop the biggest leaks for the least work.
  • Days 31 to 60: clean up Lead Source into one picklist and map the old values, add required lost reasons, and retire the old automations into one flow.
  • Days 61 to 90: rebuild campaign ROI on closed deals only, then build the Monday 5 dashboard below.

5. Three quick fixes, done during the audit

  1. Repaired the missed-call alert so the lead manager gets a text and a task within minutes.
  2. Added an automatic follow-up task, owned by the rep, the moment a deal moves to “Offer Made”.
  3. Turned on a duplicate rule that warns a rep when a seller’s phone number is already in the system.

The three fixes are agreed with you before I touch anything.
