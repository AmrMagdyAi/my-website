Dialers in Salesforce for real estate teams.

RingCentral, Aircall, Dialpad and Twilio all log calls into Salesforce, but Salesforce’s Open CTI, which many of them use, reaches end of life on February 28, 2028. Here’s how each dialer connects, which edition it needs, what a wholesaling team should log, and how to plan for 2028. The system around the calls (queues, outcomes and a caller scorecard) is in cold-calling team setup.

Short version
  • Salesforce says Open CTI reaches end of life on February 28, 2028, and existing implementations stop working after that. Salesforce Voice is the recommended replacement. source
  • Many dialer integrations use Open CTI; Twilio Flex’s docs say its Salesforce integration will stop working after that date. source
  • Edition matters: RingCentral supports Professional and above; Aircall and Dialpad require Enterprise or Unlimited. RingCentral · Aircall · Dialpad

What does the Open CTI retirement mean for an investor?

If your dialer runs inside Salesforce today, ask the vendor whether it uses Open CTI. Nothing breaks before February 28, 2028, but anything you build now should have a path past it. Dialpad, for example, says Dialpad for Salesforce is not being retired and lists Salesforce Voice or Dialpad Everywhere as options. source

  • Ask your dialer vendor, in writing, what their post-2028 Salesforce integration is.
  • Keep call outcomes in your own Salesforce fields, not only in the vendor’s objects, so reports survive a switch.
  • If you’re choosing a dialer now, weigh the vendor’s 2028 plan as much as today’s features.

Questions

Is Salesforce Open CTI being retired?

Yes. Salesforce Help says Open CTI reaches end of life on February 28, 2028, existing implementations stop working after that, and Salesforce Voice is the recommended replacement.

Which dialer works with Salesforce Professional Edition?

RingCentral lists Professional and above. Aircall and Dialpad require Enterprise or Unlimited.

Can Salesforce log texts from my dialer?

Some dialers do. Dialpad logs SMS as completed tasks on the contact’s activity history, but not on opportunities and not from shared lines. RingCentral logs calls and messages.

What is the best dialer for wholesalers using Salesforce?

It depends on your edition and volume. Check the Salesforce edition each vendor requires, how calls are logged, and the vendor’s plan after Open CTI ends in 2028.
