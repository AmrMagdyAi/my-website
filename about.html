Long bio About 130 words

Amr Magdy is a Salesforce CRM Consultant for US real estate investors, wholesalers and home-buying companies. For two years he has run the Salesforce for a two-market home-buying company in Ohio, handling every request from marketing, acquisitions, dispositions and transaction coordination. His builds include campaign ROI checked against the books, missed-call alerts on every marketing number, follow-up that stops when a seller replies, and a Transactions Board for every file under contract. Before Salesforce he spent eight years as an Adobe Certified Instructor and trained more than 8,000 people. His view is that most investor teams don't have a lead problem, they have a leak problem. He works from Cairo during US business hours and gives away the tools he uses, including the Deal Leak Score, at amrmagdy.com.
