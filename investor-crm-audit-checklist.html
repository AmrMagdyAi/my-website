The investor CRM audit checklist: 60 checks you can run yourself

This is for owners and ops leads at home-buying and wholesaling companies who want to know how well their CRM actually runs the business. It works for any CRM: Salesforce, Podio, REsimpli, GoHighLevel or an investor CRM. The checks follow a deal from the marketing that paid for it to the closing that paid you back, plus the health of the system underneath.

What to do with your no's

  1. Pick three. The three no's that cost you the most, usually in Answer, Follow-up or Spend.
  2. Write down what each one costs. Missed calls a week times your close rate times your average fee is a start. The missed call cost calculator does that math.
  3. Fix them in order, and check the list again in a month.

Questions

What should an investor CRM audit cover?

Whether every deal traces back to the marketing that paid for it, whether every seller gets answered and followed up, whether offers come from the same numbers, whether contracts become closings, whether reports can be trusted, and whether the system itself is healthy: admins, automations, duplicates and access.

Can I audit my own CRM?

Yes. Go through the 60 checks on this page with whoever runs your CRM, answering for how things work today. Then fix your three most expensive no's and check again in a month.

How often should a real estate team audit its CRM?

A full pass once or twice a year, and after any big change: a new market, new reps, a new dialer or a move to a new CRM. The weekly checks of failed automations and leads with no next step should run all the time.

Does this checklist work for Podio, REsimpli or GoHighLevel?

Yes. The checks are about outcomes, like whether every missed call gets a callback task, not about any one product's features. Some answers will just be harder to find in some tools.

What's the difference between this checklist and the CRM Revenue Audit?

The checklist is free and you run it yourself, on any CRM. The CRM Revenue Audit is done by me in your Salesforce org, for a fixed USD 1,500, and ends with what each leak is likely costing, a 30/60/90-day plan and up to three fixes done. It's only for teams already on Salesforce.

Which CRM problems should a wholesaling team check first?

The ones closest to the money: missed calls with no callback task, open leads with no next step, and deals with no source. Each one costs paid leads or makes your marketing numbers wrong.
