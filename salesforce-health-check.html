Is your Salesforce a weapon — or just a bill?

The Real Estate Salesforce Health Check is a 10-point scorecard I use at the start of every engagement. Answer the ten yes/no questions below and you'll know exactly where your CRM is leaking deals, hours, and money.

  1. Speed to lead: Does every new lead reach a rep within 5 minutes, automatically, at any hour? Fix: speed to lead guide →
  2. Lead routing: Are leads assigned by rules (round robin, market, source) instead of someone forwarding emails? Fix: lead routing →
  3. Pipeline truth: Can leadership see an accurate pipeline without asking anyone to update a spreadsheet? Fix: pipeline dashboard →
  4. Adoption: Does almost everyone log calls, notes and stage changes in Salesforce, not in their phones? Fix: adoption fixes →
  5. Automation health: Do your automations run on native Salesforce Flow rather than a tangle of third-party zaps? Fix: 15 automation recipes →
  6. Call logging: Are calls logged automatically from your phone system, with no manual entry? Fix: dialers in Salesforce →
  7. Campaign ROI: Do you know cost per lead and cost per closed deal for every channel? Fix: cost per deal calculator →
  8. Deal profitability: Can you see net profit per deal, including all costs, inside the CRM? Fix: deal profit in Salesforce →
  9. Duplicates and data: Is duplicate detection automated, and do you trust the data enough to decide from it? Fix: data quality features →
  10. Stale leads: Does the system escalate leads nobody has touched in 48 hours? Fix: report recipes →

Score: 9–10, your CRM is working for you. 6–8, solid but hours and deals are slipping. 0–5, it costs more than it returns, and every point is fixable. The PDF version is below for printing or sharing with your team.
