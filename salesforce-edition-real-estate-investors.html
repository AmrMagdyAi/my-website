Which Salesforce edition do real estate investors need?

Most real estate investing teams need Salesforce Enterprise, because it allows 200 custom objects for properties, offers and buyers, 2,000 active flows and API access for integrations. Professional can work for small teams that fit in 5 flows; Starter can’t hold property records at all. Here’s the comparison, from Salesforce’s own limits pages.

Short version
  • Starter allows 0 custom objects, so it can’t hold Property, Offer or Buyer records. Fine for a solo investor tracking contacts; not a wholesaling system. source
  • Professional / Pro Suite allows 50 custom objects but only 5 flows, and API access is an add-on for Professional (and shown as an add-on for Pro Suite on Salesforce’s pricing page), which many integrations need. source · source
  • Enterprise is where most growing investor teams land: 200 custom objects, 2,000 active flows, API included, approvals and joined reports. source
  • Don’t compare on list price. Most businesses get a discount from a Salesforce account executive based on the business and number of users.

Which edition fits which investor?

Solo investor, a few deals a year

You may not need Salesforce at all. If you do, the question is whether you need Property and Offer records; if yes, Starter won’t do it. See do I need a CRM yet? and when Salesforce is overkill.

Small team, one market, few integrations

Professional or Pro Suite can work if 5 flows cover your automation and your phone and e-signature tools support it (DocuSign and RingCentral list Professional). Count your flows before you buy.

Team with acquisitions, dispo and VAs, several lead sources

Enterprise. You’ll want more than 5 flows within months, approvals for offers above MAO, joined reports, and API access for CallRail, Google Ads conversion import, Calendly or Aircall, which require it or Enterprise outright. CallRail · Calendly

Multiple markets or a fund with investor reporting

Enterprise or Unlimited, depending on volume, sandbox needs and whether Sales Engagement cadences are worth having included.

Why do Salesforce edition names keep changing?

Salesforce repackages often. In June 2025, Agentforce 1 editions replaced Einstein 1 editions. source Third-party reports say Salesforce introduced Core, Advanced and Max editions in September 2026, with existing customers keeping their edition; I couldn’t read Salesforce’s own announcement. Questions

Which Salesforce edition is best for wholesalers?

Most wholesaling teams with several roles and lead sources fit Enterprise: 200 custom objects, 2,000 active flows and API access included. Professional can work for small teams if 5 flows and its integrations are enough.

Can Salesforce Starter track properties?

Not as separate records. Starter allows 0 custom objects, so you can’t add Property, Offer or Buyer objects.

Does Salesforce Professional include API access?

No. Salesforce says Professional can add API access as an add-on; it’s included by default in Enterprise, Unlimited, Performance and Developer.

How many flows does Professional Edition allow?

Salesforce’s allocation page for Professional lists 5 flows, versus 4,000 total and 2,000 active in Enterprise and Unlimited.

Do investors pay Salesforce list price?

Most businesses don’t. A Salesforce account executive can offer a discount based on the business and number of users, so get a quote before comparing.
