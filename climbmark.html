Plain facts

What it does, and what it doesn't

  • It is not a dialer or a CRM. It sits beside the ones you use, and can work inside GoHighLevel and Salesforce.
  • Every person, team and number in the practice is made up. Nothing in the app calls or texts a real person.
  • Team owners and team leads need two-step sign-in before they see rep results.
  • It does not promise deals. It points to the work that is slipping, and you decide what to do.
  • Anyone can delete their account and data from the account page.
