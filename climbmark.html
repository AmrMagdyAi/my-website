Plain facts

What it does, and what it doesn't

  • No new CRM and no migration. It runs on top of GoHighLevel or Salesforce, and your team keeps working where it works now.
  • It is not a dialer. Nothing in the app calls or texts anyone.
  • It does not promise deals. It shows where the money went, and you decide what to do.
  • Anyone can delete their account and data from the account page.
