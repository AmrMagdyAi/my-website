The Monday 5: five numbers to see before the week starts.

For teams that buy houses and pay for their own seller marketing. You know what these numbers mean for your business, so this page sticks to the Salesforce side: what each one needs so it's on one dashboard Monday morning, without a spreadsheet.

Cost per appointment, by campaign

What it is
Campaign spend ÷ appointments set from that campaign's leads.
In Salesforce
Actual Cost on each campaign, an Appointment Set Date on the lead, and one report grouped by campaign.
Can you pull it in under a minute?

Contracts per campaign

What it is
Signed contracts, counted by the campaign the seller first came from.
In Salesforce
The campaign copied from lead to deal and locked once the contract is signed, so a repeat seller keeps the first source.
Can you pull it in under a minute?

Contract to first seller call

What it is
Hours from “under contract” to the first logged call with the seller.
In Salesforce
A Contract Signed Date, a small flow that stamps the first completed call after it, and a list of signed deals with no call yet.
Can you pull it in under a minute?

Booked vs. kept profit

What it is
The profit expected when a deal went under contract, next to the profit kept at closing.
In Salesforce
Expected profit locked at signing, kept profit entered at closing, and a fall-out reason on every deal that didn't close.
Can you pull it in under a minute?

Campaign ROI, including the houses you keep

What it is
(Kept profit + equity in houses you kept − campaign spend) ÷ campaign spend.
In Salesforce
A “kept” flag and an equity value on the deal (after-repair value minus all-in cost), counted in each campaign's ROI.
Can you pull it in under a minute?

Questions

What are the most important KPIs for a real estate wholesaling business?

The Monday 5: cost per appointment by campaign, contracts per campaign, time from contract to first seller call, booked vs. kept profit, and campaign ROI that includes the houses you keep.

Why cost per appointment instead of cost per lead?

Lead counts include wrong numbers, tire-kickers and duplicates. An appointment means a real seller conversation, so cost per appointment is much closer to what a channel actually costs you.

Can I track these without Salesforce?

Yes, in any CRM that keeps lead source on the record from first contact to closing and logs calls with timestamps. The hard part is usually attribution: making sure the source survives from lead to contract to closed deal.
