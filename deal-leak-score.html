Questions

What is the Deal Leak Score?

A free 10-point self-check for real estate investors and wholesalers. Each yes is worth 10 points. It checks the five points where money leaks between the marketing dollar and the kept deal: Spend, Answer, Follow-up, Offer and Keep, with two checks for each.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The score is calculated in your browser. If you share a link, the link carries your ten yes/no answers and nothing else.

Why compare with my team?

Owners and acquisitions managers often answer these checks differently. Every check where your answers differ is a process that lives in someone's head instead of in the CRM, which makes it the best place to start.

Does it matter which CRM I use?

No. Every check can be set up in any investor CRM. The score measures the process, not the software.
