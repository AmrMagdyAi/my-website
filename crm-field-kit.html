The wholesaling CRM field kit.

The fields, picklists, automation rules and Monday report that keep deals from leaking between the marketing dollar and the closed deal. Copy them into Salesforce, REsimpli, Podio, HubSpot or a spreadsheet. Free to use and change.

1. Lead fields (13)

Where each lead came from, what happens next and how fast you reached the seller. The two that matter most: Lead Source required before a lead leaves New, and Next Task Date required on every open lead.

Field nameTypeRequired whenWhy it matters
Lead SourcePicklistBefore Status can leave NewWithout it, no report can tie a contract or a closed deal back to the channel that paid for it.
CampaignLookup to Campaign (or text)Before Status can leave New, when Lead Source is a paid channelLead Source says which channel; Campaign says which mailer, list or ad. Spend is entered on the campaign, so cost per appointment and ROI need this link.
MarketPicklistAlwaysLets you compare channels and reps market by market instead of mixing a strong market with a weak one.
StatusPicklistAlwaysOne shared list of stages, so a report can count how many leads sit at each step and how long they stay there.
Next Task DateDateWhile the lead is open (Status is not Dead or Under Contract)An open lead with no next date is a lead nobody is working. This field makes the gap visible and lets you block it.
Touch CountNumber (auto)Never entered by hand; counted from logged calls, texts and emails to the sellerShows how much follow-up a lead actually got before it was marked Dead or went under contract.

4. Automation rules (10)

Plain-English rules that keep the fields filled in and honest, each with a Salesforce hint and an any-CRM hint.

  • No lead leaves New without a source
  • Every logged touch counts, and the first one is stamped
  • A missed seller call becomes a callback task in 5 minutes
  • No open lead without a next date
  • Dead needs a reason
  • Dead Reason sets the revisit date
  • Offers above MAO need a second pair of eyes
  • Signing a contract stamps the numbers that matter later
  • A fall-out zeroes the fee and records why
  • Every Monday, list the hot leads nobody touched

Questions

Is it really free? Do I need to give my email?
Yes, it's free and there's no form. Download the files, copy them, change them. Credit is nice but not required.
Does it only work in Salesforce?
No. Every field and rule has a plain-English version for any CRM, including REsimpli, Podio, HubSpot or a spreadsheet. Where Salesforce needs something specific, there's a one-line Salesforce hint.
Where should I start if I can only do one thing?
Make Lead Source required before a lead leaves New, and make Next Task Date required on every open lead. Those two fields fix the most common leaks: not knowing which marketing pays, and leads nobody follows up.
Why these fields and not others?
Each one exists so a specific weekly number can be counted: cost per appointment by campaign, contracts per campaign, time from contract to first seller call, booked versus closed revenue, and ROI on kept deals.
