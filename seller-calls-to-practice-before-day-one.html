5 seller calls every new lead manager should practice before day one

These are the five calls that trip up new lead managers most. For each one: what the seller says, and what a good answer sounds like.

The five calls

Which seller calls should a new lead manager practice first?

  1. The fresh web lead. The seller filled out a form 4 minutes ago. A good call opens with who you are, why you're calling and whether now is still a good time.
  2. The price question. "Just give me a number." A good answer doesn't guess. It explains that the acquisitions manager comes back with a real offer, and asks if the seller has a number in mind.
  3. The upset seller. Someone already called them three times today. A good call says sorry once, keeps it short and asks permission to continue.
  4. The spouse. "I need to talk to my wife." A good call books a time when both can be on the phone.
  5. The no-show. The seller missed the visit. A good call is friendly, asks what came up and offers two new times.

Climbmark has practice calls for the first two (Marcus, the fresh web lead, and Gloria, who anchors on a price), with sellers who react to what the rep picks. Use the huddle format for the other three.
