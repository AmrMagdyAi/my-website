What do Salesforce licenses cost?

Salesforce bills licenses directly, and the right edition depends on your team. We pick one together on the scoping call; the edition picker is a good place to start.

Will we lose our history?

No. Every record that matters is mapped before anything moves, and we check it together after.

Will the team stop working during the switch?

No. Both systems run side by side until your team is comfortable in Salesforce.

Is Salesforce overkill for us?

Sometimes it is. If you’re a one-person shop with a few deals a month, I’ll tell you on the call and you’ll stay where you are.

How do we pay?
