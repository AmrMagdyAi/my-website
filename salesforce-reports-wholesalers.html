20 Salesforce reports for a wholesaling team.

The Salesforce reports that matter most to wholesalers show where leads come from, how fast they’re called, which offers become contracts, and what each source costs per closed deal. Here are 20 report recipes written as report type, filter, grouping and measure, the limits to know, and which five belong on the Monday dashboard.

Short version
  • These are 20 reports a wholesaling team can build in Salesforce, written as report type → filter → group by → measure, so you or your admin can build them in an afternoon.
  • Three features make them possible: custom report types (Property, Offer, Buyer), cross filters (“leads without activities”) and reporting snapshots (trends over time). source · snapshots
  • Most need fields that exist and are filled in: lead source as a picklist, call outcome, MAO, contract and assignment price, dead reason. Start with the CRM field kit.

Which reports belong on the Monday dashboard?

Five, not twenty: leads by source, speed to first contact, offers vs contracts, cost per closed deal by source, and pipeline by stage. Everything else is for drilling in when a number moves. The reasoning and formulas are in the Monday 5; layout ideas in the pipeline dashboard guide.

Why do CRM reports get ignored?

Usually because nobody trusts the data. In Validity’s 2025 survey of 602 CRM users, 76% said less than half of their CRM data is accurate and complete. source The fix is upstream: picklists instead of free text, validation rules at each stage, lead source locked after creation, and a “who changed it” report so problems are visible.

Questions

What reports should a wholesaler run in Salesforce?

Start with leads by source, speed to first contact, leads with no activity in 7 days, offers vs contracts by rep, cost per closed deal by source and pipeline by stage.

How do I report on leads with no activity in Salesforce?

Use a cross filter: leads without activities, with a date condition such as the last 7 days. Salesforce allows up to 3 cross filters per report.

Can Salesforce show trends over time?

Yes. Reporting snapshots save a report’s results to a custom object on a schedule, so you can chart weekly pipeline or lead counts.

Why can I only see 2,000 rows in a Salesforce report?

The report interface shows up to 2,000 rows. Export the report to see all rows, or summarize with groupings.
