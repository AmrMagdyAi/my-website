Stay where you are if...

  • Podio: your team knows it, someone can still change it, and the problems are messy fields rather than missing reports. A clean rebuild costs less than a switch.
  • REsimpli: you are under about five people and use the dialer, list stacking and mail every day. Replacing those means three new vendors.
  • CarrotCRM: you run Carrot websites and mainly need lead follow-up at a low price.
  • GoHighLevel: marketing and texting are the job and deals are simple. Flat pricing with unlimited users is hard to beat for that.
  • Follow Up Boss: you are an agent team more than an investor team.
  • Any tool: nobody on your side will own a new system. Switching tools won't fix ownership.

Not sure? Score your funnel with the Deal Leak Score. If the leaks are in follow-up and ownership, fix those first in the tool you have.

What usually breaks

Duplicate sellers

Stacked lists and repeat inquiries mean the same owner appears three or four times with different phone formats. Normalize phones and addresses before import, then turn on matching and duplicate rules. Duplicate rules, even ones set to allow with an alert, can make import rows fail with a DUPLICATES_DETECTED error (Salesforce Help), so read the error file after every load.

Call logs and notes

Every source cuts history short: GoHighLevel exports the latest note only, Follow Up Boss the last 50 calls, texts and notes, and Podio's Excel export carries no comments. Load what you have as completed Tasks and Notes with Data Loader, and only for open deals and active sellers. Archive the rest.

Tags

Tags come out as one cell of comma-separated text. Decide tag by tag: picklist value, campaign, or delete. Importing every tag as one huge multi-select field gives you a field nobody can report on.

Attachments

The Data Import Wizard can't load files. Data Loader can, with limits on request size (Salesforce Help). For years of photos, a link to a shared drive folder per property is often enough.

Automations

No tool exports its automations. List every Podio flow, REsimpli drip, GoHighLevel workflow or Follow Up Boss action plan, keep the ones that still matter, and rebuild them as Flows.

Original dates

Imported records get today's created date unless the admin enables the "Set Audit Fields upon Record Creation" permission (Setup > User Interface, then a permission set) or stores the original date in a custom field. Without it, your first month of reports says every lead came in on cutover day.

Import limits: the Data Import Wizard handles up to 50,000 records at a time; Data Loader handles up to 5 million and needs API access (Salesforce Help).

Risks and how to handle them

  • Leads slip during cutover. One cutover day, every source moved together, and a daily count of leads created in each system.
  • Reps keep working the old tool. Make it read-only on cutover day. A friendly week of both is how leads end up in neither.
  • Reports look wrong in month one. Expected if original dates or sources didn't move. Fix the mapping in the 50-record test, not after the full load.
  • Edition surprise. Pro Suite allows 5 flows in total. Count the automations you plan before you sign.
  • Texting compliance. If you change texting providers, your A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration may need to be moved or redone with the new provider, and your opt-out lists must come with you. Check with your provider and counsel; this page isn't legal advice.

Running both systems without losing leads

  1. Write down every lead source: each web form, each phone number, each list vendor, each texting campaign.
  2. Give each one a cutover owner and a time on cutover day.
  3. Keep the old tool open read-only so reps can look up history, not create records.
  4. Every morning for two weeks, run two counts: new leads in Salesforce yesterday, new records in the old tool yesterday. The second number should be zero.
  5. If it isn't, find the source and move it that day.
  6. Compare the Monday numbers from both systems for the first month. Where they differ, one of them is missing data.

Questions

Should I switch from Podio to Salesforce?

Switch if your team has outgrown what Podio can report or automate without a consultant, and you have someone to own the new system. Stay, or rebuild Podio, if the team knows it and the problems are messy fields and missing automations. The checklist on the moving off Podio page covers that choice.

Is REsimpli or Salesforce better for a wholesaler?

REsimpli is the better fit for a small team that wants dialer, list stacking, skip tracing and direct mail in one box with no admin. Salesforce fits a larger team with its own process, several markets, or reporting REsimpli can't produce. Many teams that switch keep their dialer and data vendors and connect them to Salesforce.

Can I keep GoHighLevel and add Salesforce?

Yes. Some teams keep GoHighLevel for marketing and texting and make Salesforce the record of every deal. The risk is duplicates: decide which system creates the person and sync on one key, usually phone number, with a duplicate rule on the Salesforce side.

Will I lose my call history and notes?

Some of it, often. GoHighLevel's contact export carries only the latest note, Follow Up Boss caps calls, texts and notes at the last 50 per contact, and Podio's Excel export leaves out comments and files. Move history for open deals and active sellers, and keep the old account read-only for a few months for lookups.

How long does a switch take?

The plan on this page runs 90 days, with new leads flowing into Salesforce by about day 45. Clean data and few integrations make it faster; five tools and three years of tags make it slower.

How do I avoid losing leads during the switch?

Pick one cutover day for new leads, move every web form and phone number on that day, and run a daily check for two weeks: leads created in each system yesterday. If the old system got any, a source was missed.
