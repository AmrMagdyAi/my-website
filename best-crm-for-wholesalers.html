Best CRM for wholesalers in 2026.

There isn’t one best CRM for wholesalers. There’s a best one for your team size and the tools you already use. Below are 11 options compared on the things that matter for wholesaling, with every feature and price linked to the vendor’s own site.

Short version
  • Want calling, texting, lists, skip tracing and buyer sites in one login? Look at the all-in-one investor CRMs: REsimpli, FreedomSoft, REI BlackBook.
  • Already have a dialer and data, and want strict follow-up workflows? InvestorFuse / CarrotCRM.
  • Want to build your own process as you grow past a few reps or markets? Salesforce, with setup help.
  • BatchLeads and DealMachine are data tools, not pipeline CRMs. Follow Up Boss and HubSpot aren’t built for investors.

Disclosure: I build Salesforce CRMs for investors, so I have a bias. To keep it in check, every feature and price below comes from the vendor’s own site, linked in the row. I don’t list Salesforce prices: most businesses get a discount from a Salesforce account executive based on the business and number of users.

REsimpli: dialer, data and buyer tools in one login

REsimpli calls itself “built for every kind of real estate investor.” Its pricing page says every feature is on every plan and you only pick the team size. You get a triple-line dialer, list stacking, skip tracing credits, seller and buyer sites, cash buyer search and a KPI dashboard. REsimpli features · pricing

  • Good fit: a team that wants data, dialing and follow-up in one place and wants as little setup as possible.
  • Watch for: unlimited calling and texting are “under our fair use policy.” Users are capped at 4 on Solo and 12 on Team, and AI calling is billed per minute. REsimpli blog
  • Price: Solo $299 a month monthly or $249 annual; Team $899; Business $2,199. Published price, read from the vendor’s site in October 2026. Prices change; check the link.

FreedomSoft: all-in-one with metered usage

FreedomSoft lists list building, skip tracing, a cold-call dialer, texting, direct mail, a website builder, a KPI dashboard and dispositions. Texts ($0.04), voice minutes and skip tracing (from $0.12 a result) are billed on top of the plan. Zapier is on Grow and Scale. FreedomSoft pricing

  • Good fit: a team that sends direct mail and wants it in the same system as calls and texts.
  • Watch for: user and website caps on Start and Grow; extra users are Scale-only.
  • Price: $197, $297 or $797 a month ($147, $247 or $597 annual). Published price, read from the vendor’s site in October 2026. Prices change; check the link.

REI BlackBook: the low-cost wholesaler CRM

REI BlackBook calls itself “the CRM built for wholesalers.” Every plan includes the Profit Dial dialer and a property pipeline with disposition tools; the wholesaler page describes a seller site and a cash-buyer site (Basic includes 1 website, Growth 3). REI BlackBook pricing · wholesalers page

  • Good fit: a solo wholesaler or small team starting out.
  • Watch for: Basic caps you at 500 contacts and 1 user. Texts and calls are metered after the included credits.
  • Price: $97, $197 or $297 a month. Published price, read from the vendor’s site in October 2026. Prices change; check the link.

InvestorFuse (now also CarrotCRM): follow-up discipline

Carrot owns InvestorFuse and sells the same software as CarrotCRM. It’s built around workflows that tell each rep the next action, plus automated KPIs and a rep scorecard. It doesn’t describe a built-in dialer, list building or skip tracing; calls come through partners like smrtPhone. InvestorFuse features · Carrot

  • Good fit: an acquisitions team that already has data and a dialer and keeps losing leads in follow-up.
  • Watch for: a setup fee (amount not shown) and AI features billed per use.
  • Price: Essentials $147, Pro $247, Premium Beta $377 (shown down from $497). Published price, read from the vendor’s site in October 2026. Prices change; check the link.

Podio: cheap and flexible, but you build it

Podio is a general work-management tool. Investors usually start from the Real Estate Investment App Pack, which has seller leads, cash buyers, contracts and more than 50 automation flows. Calls, texts and data all come from third parties. app pack · pricing

  • Good fit: a tinkerer on a tight budget.
  • Watch for: once you upgrade, you pay for every employee in your workspaces.
  • Price: Free, then Plus $11.20 and Premium $19.20 per user a month, annual. Published price, read from the vendor’s site in October 2026. Prices change; check the link.

GoHighLevel: marketing first, wholesaling not built in

HighLevel is built for agencies and small businesses. Its real estate page talks to agents and brokerages, and the Property Investor bundle is templates and funnels. Skip tracing, list stacking and cash buyer search aren’t built in (not listed on HighLevel’s pages). Phone, text and AI are billed by usage. real estate page · investor playbook · pricing

  • Good fit: a team that lives on funnels, ads and text campaigns and has someone to build workflows.
  • Watch for: usage fees on top of the plan, and A2P 10DLC registration fees for texting. phone pricing
  • Price: $97, $297 or $497 a month plus usage. Published price, read from the vendor’s site in October 2026. Prices change; check the link.

Salesforce: build your own process

Salesforce isn’t a wholesaling product out of the box. You (or someone like me) build Property, Offer, Buyer and Assignment records, the automations and the reports. Dialers and texting come from add-ons or AppExchange apps, and data from tools like BatchLeads, which lists a Salesforce integration. Salesforce objects · Sales Dialer · BatchLeads

  • Good fit: teams with several reps, markets or entities who want their own process, deeper reporting and a system that can grow with them.
  • Watch for: it needs setup. Starter allows 0 custom objects, so a Property record needs Professional or higher. Starter limits · Professional limits
  • Price: quoted. Most businesses get a discount from a Salesforce account executive based on the business and number of users. The edition picker tells you what to ask for.

BatchLeads and DealMachine: data tools, not CRMs

BatchLeads (owned by PropStream since July 2025) does list stacking, enrichment and driving for dollars, and has no deal pipeline. DealMachine moved to per-user plans with data credits in July 2026, and its own comparison lists SMS as not included. Most teams pair either one with a CRM. PropStream acquisition · DealMachine pricing

Follow Up Boss and HubSpot: built for someone else

Follow Up Boss is built for agent teams: great routing and texting, no list or dispositions tools. HubSpot is a solid small-business CRM, but a custom Property object needs the Enterprise tier. Follow Up Boss · HubSpot objects More: Salesforce vs Follow Up Boss.

How to choose in 15 minutes

  • Count the logins. One to three people: an all-in-one is usually enough. Five or more reps, VAs or markets: look harder at reporting, permissions and custom records.
  • List the tools you’ll keep. If you love your dialer and data provider, you don’t need them bundled.
  • Price the usage, not the plan. Texts, minutes, skip tracing and AI are billed on top for most of these.
  • Ask how you get your data out. Export, API or Zapier. You’ll care the day you switch.
  • Test one deal end to end in the trial. Lead in, call, offer, contract, buyer, assignment. Where you hit copy-paste is your answer.

Questions

What is the best CRM for wholesalers in 2026?

It depends on team size and what you already use. All-in-one investor CRMs like REsimpli, FreedomSoft and REI BlackBook bundle calling, texting and lists. InvestorFuse focuses on follow-up workflows. Salesforce fits teams that want their own process and deeper reporting and are willing to set it up.

What is the cheapest CRM for wholesaling?

Podio has a free plan and paid plans from $11.20 per user a month on annual billing, but you build everything yourself. REI BlackBook starts at $97 a month with a 500-contact cap. These are published prices from October 2026.

Is GoHighLevel good for wholesaling?

It handles funnels, texting and workflows well, but skip tracing, list stacking and cash buyer search aren’t built in, and calls and texts are billed by usage. Its real estate page targets agents and brokerages.

Is Salesforce too much for a small wholesaler?

Often, yes. A solo wholesaler closing a few deals a month is usually fine on an investor CRM. Salesforce starts making sense with several reps, markets or entities, or when you need reports your current CRM can’t do.

Is BatchLeads a CRM?

Not really. BatchLeads is a data and outreach tool with list stacking, enrichment and click-to-dial. It has no deal pipeline, so most teams connect it to a CRM.

How much does Salesforce cost for a wholesaler?

I don’t quote list prices. Most businesses get a discount from a Salesforce account executive based on the business and number of users. Use the edition picker to work out which edition to ask about.
