Salesforce vs GoHighLevel for investors.

GoHighLevel is a marketing platform with a CRM inside. Salesforce is a CRM platform you shape to your process. Neither has wholesaling data built in. Here’s how they compare, with every claim linked to the source.

Short version
  • GoHighLevel is a marketing platform: funnels, sites, texting, calling and workflows, billed by plan plus usage.
  • Salesforce is a CRM platform you configure: custom records, reporting, permissions and a large app marketplace.
  • Neither has wholesaling data (lists, skip tracing, cash buyers) built in. Both need a data tool next to them.

Disclosure: I build Salesforce CRMs for investors, so I have a bias. To keep it in check, every feature and price below comes from the vendor’s own site, linked in the row. I don’t list Salesforce prices: most businesses get a discount from a Salesforce account executive based on the business and number of users.

GoHighLevel’s usage fees

HighLevel bills phone and text by usage on top of the plan. Its help center lists US rates per minute and per text segment, plus A2P 10DLC registration and monthly campaign fees for texting. Budget for these at your volume, not just the plan price. HighLevel phone pricing

Where GoHighLevel wins

  • Marketing in one place. Funnels, sites, texting, email and calling in one tool.
  • Predictable plan price for the platform itself, with unlimited users on Starter.
  • Fast to launch if you use a template or snapshot.

Where Salesforce wins

  • Data model. A deal with several offers, a property with several sellers, a buyer with a buy box, each a related record with its own validation and automation.
  • Reporting and audit trail. Who changed the offer price, and when. 37 features investors use
  • Integrations. DocuSign, QuickBooks, dialers and data tools. Integrations guide
  • Governance. Sandboxes to test changes, permissions by team and market.

Running both

Some teams keep GoHighLevel for funnels and texting campaigns and send leads into Salesforce as the system of record. It works if you decide which system owns each field and sync only one way for most of them. Otherwise you get two versions of every seller.

Questions

Is GoHighLevel a good CRM for real estate investors?

It’s strong for funnels, texting and workflows. Skip tracing, list stacking and cash buyer search aren’t built in, and its real estate page targets agents and brokerages, so wholesalers usually add a data tool.

Is Salesforce better than GoHighLevel?

They solve different problems. GoHighLevel is a marketing platform; Salesforce is a CRM platform you configure. Salesforce is the better system of record when you need custom records, reports and permissions across a growing team.

How much does GoHighLevel cost?

HighLevel’s published plans in October 2026 are Starter $97, Unlimited $297 and Agency Pro $497 a month, with phone, text, email and AI billed by usage on top.

Can GoHighLevel and Salesforce work together?

Yes. One setup sends leads from GoHighLevel funnels into Salesforce. Decide which system owns each field before you connect them.
