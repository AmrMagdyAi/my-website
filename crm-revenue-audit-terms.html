CRM Revenue Audit · scope and terms

What you’re agreeing to

Plain terms for the fixed-price audit. If anything here doesn’t fit how you work, tell me before we start and we’ll sort it out.

Amr Magdy CRM Consulting · info@AmrMagdy.com

Who it’s for

Real estate investor, wholesaler and home-buying teams already on Salesforce. On another CRM? See moving to Salesforce instead.

What’s reviewed

Marketing and campaign ROI, speed to lead, acquisitions and follow-up, data health, automations and integrations, and the reports and dashboards you run the business on.

What you get

  1. A written findings report: every issue, where it is, what it’s likely costing you and how big the fix is.
  2. A 30 / 60 / 90 roadmap with the fixes in order and rough effort for each.
  3. A field-by-field spec for a “Monday 5” owner dashboard.
  4. Up to 3 small, safe fixes made during the audit, each agreed with you first.
  5. A kick-off call and a recorded findings walkthrough with your team.

What I need from you

  • A 45-minute kick-off call.
  • A read-only Salesforce login (I send simple steps).
  • A yes or no on each quick fix.
  • A 60-minute walkthrough call.

Not included

Building fixes beyond the quick fixes (up to 3), data migration and new integrations. Those are scoped separately if you want them.

Fee and payment

$1,500 USD, fixed. 50% to start and 50% when the report is delivered, by bank transfer or card through an invoice. Escrow through an Upwork contract is available on request; the payment is then held until you approve the report.

If you hire me for the build within 30 days of the walkthrough, the full $1,500 comes off that project.

My promise

If the report doesn’t give you at least five specific, fixable issues worth more to you than the fee, say so on the walkthrough call and I’ll refund it in full.

Founding clients

The first three audit clients get a direct WhatsApp line to me during the audit and for 30 days after the walkthrough. In return I ask for four things: a short case study written with you and published only with your OK, a review on Upwork or LinkedIn at handover, a two-line quote in your own words, and permission to count your findings, without your name, in the totals I publish.

Access and your data

I work from a read-only user and change nothing without your OK. The user only gets edit access while I make the quick fixes you approve. It uses one of your Salesforce licenses; if none is spare, we free one together or do the review on screen shares with whoever runs Salesforce on your side. Your data stays in your org. I don’t copy it out except what the report needs, and I keep it confidential. I’m happy to sign your NDA before I log in. Remove my login any time after the walkthrough.

Urgent findings

If I find something actively losing leads, like an alert that silently stopped, I tell you the same day.

Agreeing

Paying the first invoice, or accepting the Upwork contract, means you accept these terms.
