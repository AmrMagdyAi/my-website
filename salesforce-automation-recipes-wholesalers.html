15 automation recipes for a wholesaling CRM.

The automations that pay off first in a wholesaling CRM are a call task for every new lead, a callback for every missed call, required fields before a lead is passed, and long follow-up for “not now” leads. Here are 15 recipes written as trigger → action, which Salesforce tool to use for each, and the order to build them.

Short version
  • These are the automations I’d build first for a wholesaling team, written as trigger → action so you can hand them to whoever builds your CRM.
  • Count them before you pick an edition: Salesforce Professional allows 5 flows; Enterprise allows 2,000 active. source · source
  • Several are better as validation rules, approval processes or dashboard subscriptions than as flows.

Which automations should you build first?

Week 1

New lead task, missed call callback, warm/hot validation. These protect the leads you already pay for. See speed to lead and the handoff checklist.

Week 2

No activity sweep, not-now re-contact, dead reason. These protect the long follow-up where most conversions happen.

Week 3

Contract to dispo, buyer match, closing checklist. These shorten days to assignment.

Week 4

Offer approvals, fall-outs out of ROI, Monday dashboard. These make the numbers honest.

What goes wrong with CRM automation?

  • Building automations before the fields are clean: a flow that reads a free-text lead source does nothing useful.
  • Too many alerts: if every change pings someone, people mute them. Alert on exceptions only.
  • No owner: every automation should have a person who gets told when it fails.
  • Overlapping tools: a Zapier chain and a flow both creating tasks doubles the work. See replacing Zapier chains.

Questions

What should a wholesaler automate first in a CRM?

The new-lead call task, a callback task for missed calls, and required fields before a lead is marked warm or hot. They protect leads you already paid for.

How many flows can Salesforce Professional run?

Salesforce’s Professional Edition allocation page lists 5 flows. Enterprise and Unlimited allow 4,000 total and 2,000 active.

Should required fields be a flow or a validation rule?

A validation rule. It blocks the save everywhere, including imports and integrations, and can be conditional on stage or status.
