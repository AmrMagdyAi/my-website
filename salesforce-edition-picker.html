Questions

Which Salesforce edition do I need as a wholesaler?

If you need Property, Offer or Buyer records, more than 5 automations, or API integrations like CallRail or Google Ads conversion import, start from Enterprise. Small teams with few automations can start on Professional.

Does this tool show Salesforce prices?

No. Most businesses get a discount from a Salesforce account executive based on the business and number of users, so the tool gives you questions to ask instead of list prices.
